Key to symbols and expressions

Show key
k
Number of dimensions (variables) in the distribution.
X₁ … Xk
The k random variables. Each is normally distributed on its own.
f(x)
Probability density: how concentrated the probability is near the point x. Higher means more likely nearby.
μ (mean)
The center of the distribution, one value per dimension. Shown as a dot or cross on the plots.
σ (spread)
Standard deviation of a single variable: its typical distance from its mean.
ρ (correlation)
How strongly two variables move together, from −1 to 1. Positive tilts the shape up and to the right, negative tilts it down; 0 means uncorrelated.
Σ (covariance matrix)
Table of all variances (diagonal, σ²) and covariances (off-diagonal, ρσσ) that together set the shape and tilt.
Cov(X,Y)
Covariance of two variables: ρ × σₓ × σᵧ.
|Σ| (det)
Determinant of Σ. A larger value means the distribution occupies more volume and is more spread out.
Peak density
Height of f at the mean, the highest point of the distribution: 1 / ((2π)k/2√|Σ|).
1σ, 2σ, 3σ
Ellipses (2-D) or ellipsoid shells (3-D) of constant density at 1, 2 and 3 standard deviations from the mean (Mahalanobis distance). In one dimension these hold about 68%, 95% and 99.7% of the probability.
Marginal
The distribution of a chosen subset of variables with all the others ignored. Any pair's marginal is itself a bivariate normal.
Conditional
The distribution of two chosen variables given that every other variable is held at a fixed value.
Pairwise grid
Matrix of plots: each variable's own curve on the diagonal, each pair's marginal below it, and correlations above it.
Wall projections
In the 3-D cube, the flat outlines on the back walls show each pair's marginal, as if the ellipsoid cast a shadow onto the wall.
Positive definite
The condition that Σ describes a valid distribution. The page limits correlations so that this always holds.
