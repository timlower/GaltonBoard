Remember: regression describes association, not causation, and its p-values assume a roughly linear relationship with evenly scattered errors.

Residual plot

A healthy model leaves residuals as a shapeless band around zero. A curve means the relationship is not linear; a fan means the noise is not constant; isolated points far from zero are outliers.

Simple regression

X, Y
The predictor and the outcome being predicted.
Ŷ (Y-hat)
The value of Y predicted by the fitted line.
b₀, b₁
Intercept (predicted Y when X = 0) and slope (change in predicted Y per 1-unit increase in X).
Pearson r
Strength and direction of the linear association, from −1 to +1.
R²
Share of the variation in Y accounted for by the model.
s
Residual standard error: the typical size of a prediction miss, in Y units.
Std. error of slope
How much the slope would vary from sample to sample.
t, p
t = slope / its standard error; p is the chance of a slope this far from 0 if the true slope were 0.
Confidence band
Plausible range for the average Y at each X.
Prediction band
Plausible range for a single new Y at each X (wider).

Multiple regression

X1, X2
The two predictors; Y is the outcome.
Estimate
Coefficient: change in Y per 1-unit increase in that predictor, holding the other constant.
Std. error, t, p
Uncertainty of each coefficient, its t statistic, and its p-value.
Adjusted R²
R² penalized for the number of predictors, so extra predictors must earn their place.
F, p (F test)
Tests whether the predictors together explain more than chance.
Residual df
n minus the number of estimated coefficients (including the intercept).
VIF
Variance inflation factor: how much the other predictor inflates this coefficient's variance. Above about 5 signals multicollinearity.
Alone
The slope from regressing Y on that predictor by itself, ignoring the other.
True value
The effect used to generate the data, rescaled to the plot's units.
Partial plot
Y and one predictor after removing the other's influence; its slope equals that coefficient.

Both modes

Residual
Actual Y minus fitted Y; the part the model misses.
n
Number of points.
Variance bar
Blue = explained (R²); red = left in the residuals.
