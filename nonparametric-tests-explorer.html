Deciding which to use

Rank and count-based tests suit ordinal scores, small samples that look non-normal, and data with outliers or heavy skew. When the scores are interval-level, roughly normal and free of outliers, the t test is more powerful; the efficiencies quoted below show what is given up. Answer a few questions to choose among the tests on this page.
